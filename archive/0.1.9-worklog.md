# 0.1.9 worklog — 2026-10-08

## Prompt (draft)
The trajectory forms well, but touching a magnet or reflector looks like a recalculation with very
big changes even when the instrument barely moved — suspected bug. Also: add a speed slider next to
pause, from the current speed up to 50x.

## Diagnosis (measured, not guessed)
- **A small edit swaps the orbit at some phases and does nothing at others.** A 2 px magnet nudge
  (or a 0.05 rad reflector nudge) applied at t = 3, 12 and 20 s moved the committed rail by
  46–62 px mean / 136–150 px max and left the beam 58–86 px off it — outside the 40 px pull
  tolerance, so guidance went to exactly zero and the beam flew free. The beam path diverged from
  the unedited run by up to **316 px** (mean 54–97 px) over the next 15 s. At t = 8, 30, 40 s the
  same nudge changed nothing. Both regimes are visible in `experiments/edit-continuity.js`.
- **Root cause: the forced re-fit searches the wrong forecast.** `predict(force)` re-derived the
  rail from the unguided forecast of the beam's *live* state. The live state has been pulled onto
  the rail, and the unguided field does not reproduce the rail, so that forecast's best
  near-recurrence is regularly a *different* lap: measured best-pair periods 7.60–8.43 s against a
  held 8.00–8.28 s, with the pair's lap 105–161 px from the held rail. Committing it swapped the
  orbit; the pair flickering in and out of existence during a drag made the rail rewrite on
  40–55 of 60 drag events.
- **Gating the fix on detection tolerances does not work.** A held lap already sits near them: the
  transported seam is at 29° of the 45° direction limit and 63 px of the 100 px closure limit at
  *zero* edit, so a 0.04 rad reflector nudge flipped the decision and re-fitted anyway. Detection
  limits judge whether a lap is worth committing, not whether an edit is small.
- **The unstitched lap is not the rail the beam rides.** Judging the transported lap before
  stitching accepted rails the beam was 79–94 px from, because the seam stitch moves the rail by
  up to the closure gap.

## Development
- `js/reactor.js`
  - `retainLoop()`: transports the held lap through an edited field — propagate the rail's own
    entry state for one period, stitch the seam, and keep that lap while the beam still meets the
    transported rail inside the pull tolerances (`findLoopAnchor` on the stitched rail). Called
    from `commitDecision` on a field edit; when it fails, the previous search path is unchanged.
  - `stitchSeam(rail, count)` extracted from `commitLoop` so a transported lap can be stitched
    before it is judged and before it is committed (the second stitch inside `commitLoop` is then
    a no-op).
  - `forecastInto(next, guidance, from, samples)`: forecasts an arbitrary state (the rail entry)
    with a capped horizon instead of always the live beam over the full 14 s.
  - `commitLoop(route, i, j)` takes explicit indices; `findLoopAnchor(rail, p, out)` takes the
    rail, so it can judge the committed loop or a transported lap.
  - `forecastInterval` (default `FORECAST_PERIOD`, now exported): the refresh cadence in
    simulation seconds, stretched by the UI speed so fast-forward does not multiply the
    per-real-second cost of the two forecast passes.
  - Detection radius/cosine hoisted into `LOOP_DETECT_RADIUS2` / `LOOP_DETECT_COSINE`.
- `js/app.js`, `index.html`, `style.css`
  - **Speed** slider in the transport bar next to Pause (1×–50×, step 1). `setSpeed` scales the
    frame accumulator and `sim.forecastInterval`; Reset restores 1×.
- `tests/reactor.test.js`: 5 new tests — re-deriving with no field change reproduces the rail
  exactly; a small edit transports the rail (worst sample moves < 12 px, period within 0.25 s,
  beam still riding); a sub-pixel edit does not reshape the locked trajectory (worst drift
  < 30 px); an edit that breaks the lap lets the search re-derive; the refresh interval stretches
  with the speed. `commitLoop` call updated to the new signature.
- `experiments/edit-continuity.js` (new): rail/beam divergence per edit size and phase, the
  large-edit boundary, and drag emulation (offset per event, engagement).
- `experiments/ui-wiring.js` (new): minimal DOM stub that loads `index.html`'s scripts and drives
  the transport controls — the Playwright smoke test has no browser binary here.

  `experiments/ui-wiring.js` stands in for it.

## Limitations and next step
- After a large edit the beam rides the transported lap looser than a freshly searched one. Over
  t55–80 after the documented 60 px + 0.7 rad perturbation: recurrence median **8.5 px** vs
  0.8 px before, rail offset median 22.1 px vs 8.4 px (p90 37.9 px against the 40 px tolerance),
  still 100% locked, bounded and finite. Continuity is bought with re-lock tightness. A sub-pixel
  nudge *after* such an edit stays continuous in 3 of 4 measured phases (drift ≤ 3 px) and
  re-derives once (41 px mean).
- A drag that pushes the beam past the pull tolerance hands the rail back to the search once; the
  beam re-locks within about a second (offset 39 px → 7 px in the traced drag).
- The transported rail keeps the held lap's period, so an edit that changes the natural period a
  lot eventually fails the anchor test and re-derives — the intended boundary, measured at
  60–140 px magnet moves.
- **Suggested next step:** playtest the speed slider's feel at 10–50× (trail length, target bars,
  how long a fast-forward feels), then resume the stationary-lap preset search from §0.1.8.
