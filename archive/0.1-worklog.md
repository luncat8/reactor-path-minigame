## Limitations and next step
- Initial duties and control ranges are provisional; no winning preset or scenario-solvability claim.
- Forecast swaps and attraction feedback still need quantitative convergence/drift measurement. Dwell geometry approximates curved and reflected motion between samples with chords.
- Mobile layout works; fine path tuning is best on a larger screen, with panel buttons/sliders available for small touch targets.

**Suggested next step:** add a deterministic tuning experiment that measures target duties, cooling exposure, and forecast/live drift across magnet settings; use it to build and playtest one verified solvable scenario before adding levels or persistence.
