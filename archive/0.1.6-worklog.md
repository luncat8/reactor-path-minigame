# 0.1.6 worklog — 2026-10-06

## Prompt (draft)
The previous three attempts still selected the wrong trajectory point and did not show the intended
target in debug. Expected: a green target circle on the trajectory, target and current particle
movement vectors, and a force from the particle that gently matches both the target position and
movement state to form a closed loop. Reconsider whether past or future trajectory points should be
eligible, and avoid forces that attach to the wrong branch of a self-crossing path.

## Diagnosis and decision
- The committed-route implementation solved a different problem: its true nearest projection was
  usually the immediate trajectory beside the beam. That is mathematically nearest but cannot close
  a loop, so the green ring correctly appeared almost on the particle and guidance was mostly idle.
- Lateral-only steering deliberately removed speed matching. It could reduce cross-track error but
  could not put the particle into the same full movement state as a return pass.
- Position-only crossing selection is insufficient. A spatially closer opposing branch, or a branch
  with very different speed, is not a useful loop target.
- **Only future states are now eligible.** A past state acts as a history rail after a player edit and
  lets the just-traversed outgoing branch compete with the intended next traversal. The selected
  future return state says where and how the particle should rejoin the orbit.

## Development
- `js/reactor.js`
  - Replaced committed-route anchors, local projection windows, look-ahead pursuit, and route memory
    with a future-return phase-state search.
  - Forecast buffers now store `vx`/`vy` at every sample. Candidate points interpolate position,
    velocity, and time on trajectory segments.
  - The next 2 seconds are excluded by default; influence fades in over the following second.
  - Selection minimizes combined position and velocity error. Opposing and perpendicular candidates
    are rejected before scoring, preventing wrong-way crossing capture.
  - New bounded state controller combines `0.5 × position error` with configurable target-minus-live
    velocity matching. Distance, heading, temporal fade, and overall strength scale the result.
  - Forecasting uses two reusable passes: one proposes the route and the second follows it. This
    removes the stale committed route without allocating in the hot path.
  - Live debug is recomputed for the displayed post-step particle, so target, vectors, force, and

## Limitations and next step
- Phase-space weighting (0.75 seconds converting velocity error to position units), position gain,
  and default movement gain are initial gameplay values. The deterministic probe shows substantially
  better recurrence, not an optimal or guaranteed closed orbit for every magnet arrangement.
- Full-state matching can legitimately accelerate or brake; unlike the removed lateral-only model,
  “never backwards” is not an invariant. Safety comes from future/heading compatibility, smooth
  fades, reach, and a 50-unit acceleration cap.
- Scenario target duties and cooling remain uncalibrated; no winning preset is claimed.

**Suggested next step:** visually playtest target handoffs while dragging magnets, then sweep exclusion
delay, movement gain, and phase-state weighting against recurrence, target duty, and cooling to pick
one calibrated default and a verified solvable field preset.
