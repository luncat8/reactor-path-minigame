# 0.1.11 worklog — 2026-10-08

## Prompt (draft)
"implement" — the `## gameplay` section of `draft.txt`:
- target fuel circles: beam crossing heats the whole reactor (show heat level) and burns fuel (show remaining fuel bar). Low fuel may be removed by click. Button places a new fuel circle at a random location, max 4.
- cooler circles: beam crossing cools the reactor; the removed heat is the output heat. Output heat per time is scored against a target value that drifts slowly at random. Coolers may be removed by click; button places a new one at a random location.

  fuel circles), which is why the target range is 2–8 %/s.
