03 / TRAJECTORY GUIDANCE

The guide detects a near-closed lap in the unguided forecast — the beam's own next return, almost meeting itself — commits it as a loop rail, and stitches the small seam shut. The force then works like a magnetic guide rail: it pulls the beam toward the nearest compatible section of the loop and matches the local speed and heading, so the lap repeats and the orbit closes. The pull acts only inside the distance and direction tolerances and fades smoothly to zero at them, so a crossing branch moving the wrong way can never capture the beam. While the beam rides the rail it is held; a lost rail re-locks after a short grace. In debug: green closed curve = committed loop rail, green ring and arrow = aim point on the rail, cyan arrow = current movement, dashed line = offset to the rail, white arrow = actual matching force. Set strength or reach to 0 to disable.
