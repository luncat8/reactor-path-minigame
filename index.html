03 / TRAJECTORY GUIDANCE

The beam is steered onto the nearest forward-aligned point of its committed route — the plan it announced, held for the selected memory — and aims one forward-aim interval along it. Only the lateral part of that correction is applied, so guidance can never thrust or brake the beam; speed stays with speed regulation. Distance fades to zero at the selected reach, heading mismatch fades to zero at the selected angle, and an opposing or perpendicular pass is ignored. A beam already on its route therefore feels almost no push, and the debug arrow scales by the square root of the force — the readout is the exact value. Set strength or reach to 0 to disable.
