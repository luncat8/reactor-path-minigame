## Diagnosis (measured, not guessed)
- **Sweep at the requested 40 px tolerance locked nothing.** Strength 0.4–1.5 × pull 40/60/80/100 px:
  only 60–100 px pulls closed loops; every 40 px row stayed at the guidance-off baseline
  (median recurrence ~107 px, locked < 10%).
- **Seam hypothesis confirmed.** Committed laps have closure errors with median 52 px (p75 72,
  p90 88) — wider than the 40 px pull. The beam therefore crossed every junction unguided, drifted,
  and the beam-anchored detection simply re-committed around the drift: the rail followed the beam
  instead of correcting it (95% of commits beam-anchored, recurrence identical to baseline).
- **Tightening detection does not fix it.** Detection at 40 px/30° drops the rail half the time
  (held 50–56%), and recurrence still stays at baseline.
- **Tail-concentrated stitch was not enough.** Blending the closure error into the last 35% of the
  lap created a localized bend the beam escaped from; 40 px pulls still failed while 80 px pulls
  locked (median 12 px).
- **Rail hijack.** Frame traces showed the beam riding a period-8.3 loop perfectly (offset 0–2 px)
  and then a refresh committing a different period-7.8 loop family 170–530 px away: every refresh
  re-committed the globally best-scoring pair, so an unrelated recurrence stole the rail mid-ride.

## Development
- `js/reactor.js`
  - **Seam stitch.** `commitLoop` blends the closure error (position and velocity) linearly over the
    whole lap, so the committed rail is one closed curve and sample count−1 lands exactly on
    sample 0. Whole-lap linear blending beats a tail-concentrated smoothstep bend: it is nearly a
    constant-velocity bias, so the rail stays close to the field's own dynamics everywhere.
  - **Ride hysteresis.** `commitDecision`: while the beam is within pull tolerance of the rail the
    committed shape is frozen and only the grace timer refreshes — no other candidate can hijack
    the ride. Re-fit happens only after the beam has stayed off the rail past the 1.2 s grace.
  - **Capture guard.** An unrelated (not beam-anchored) loop may only be committed when the
    forecast shows the beam reaching its entry within 6 s.
  - **Field-edit re-fit.** `predict(force)` bypasses the ride freeze so a player magnet/reflector
    edit re-derives the rail from the new field immediately, even mid-ride (or paused).
  - Default `guideStrength` raised 0.65 → 0.9: the sweep's smallest strength that locks loops at
    the requested 40 px / 30° tolerances.

## Limitations and next step

  layouts are not swept; a pathological field may offer no near-recurrence and the guide stays idle.
- The locked default loop does not feed all three targets evenly (A −1%, B +80%, C +24%):

  No winning preset is shipped yet.
- Strength 0.9 is the tuned default, not a physical constant; pull tolerance below ~30 px will not
  lock because lap-to-lap drift exceeds it.
